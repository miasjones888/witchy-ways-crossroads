## XI. The Practitioner-Search Recommendation for the Keyed Reader

Drawing the verifications and the female-led trauma directory work together into a working recommendation for the keyed reader's actual practice:

**For the trauma-aware ancestral work** (Ch 5 build directive): the Foor-trained Ancestral Medicine practitioner directory is the most directly relevant tool. Search by "settler colonial" or "racialized intergenerational" or "ancestors of slavery" specialization. Multiple intake calls with several candidates. Attention to whether the practitioner can hold both the descended-from-the-enslaved dimension and the descended-from-the-enslaver dimension (through the Dutch ancestor) without flattening either.

**For the somatic body work** (Ch 5 excavation tier): Cultural Somatics Institute training is the major contemporary apparatus, but is community/group-oriented. Individual practitioner work for the keyed reader's specific configuration — somatic experiencing or sensorimotor psychotherapy with racialized-trauma awareness — is best located through Inclusive Therapists with appropriate filters (female practitioner; doctoral level; somatic experiencing or sensorimotor; trauma specialization; identity awareness). Multiple intake calls. The relationship work selects the practitioner.

**For the Welsh devotional engagement** (Ch 4 ancestral dimension): Sisterhood of Avalon's Seeds of Devotion immersion is the most directly inheritance-consonant program (focused on the five Welsh goddesses including Rhiannon). Anglesey Druid Order's training program is the alternate pathway; the order operates from Anglesey itself and has the geographic-cultural rooting Sisterhood of Avalon does not. OBOD is the broader cross-tradition Druidic option. The choice depends on the keyed reader's actual call — Sisterhood of Avalon if the deity-devotional Rhiannon work is primary; Anglesey Druid Order if the Welsh-tradition-and-place Druidic work is primary; OBOD if a broader cross-tradition Druidic engagement is appropriate.

**For the Continental Hermetic-Masonic engagement** (Ch 4 ancestral dimension; Ch 5 commitment layer): Le Droit Humain American Federation is the most directly inheritance-consonant accessible body for the keyed reader given Continental tradition specifically and gender access. Direct contact with the American Federation surfaces specific lodge locations and initiation procedures.

**For the Carolinas Gullah Geechee and broader hoodoo work** (Ch 4 ancestral dimension): the cultural-political infrastructure (Gullah/Geechee Sea Island Coalition, Penn Center, the Cultural Heritage Corridor) provides cultural-historical engagement. Spiritual practitioner relationships develop in community over years rather than through directory search. The Beaucoup Hoodoo Fest (annual, New Orleans) is a possible entry point for sustained Black-led hoodoo / Vodou / African Diaspora community engagement.

**For the genealogical research dimension** (across Ch 4 and Ch 5): direct engagement with **Nicka Sewell-Smith** specifically for the Jones-on-Jones question (whether the keyed reader's mother's family owned her father's). This is precisely the kind of work she specializes in.
