5. **Altered states / trance work** from § IV split between Ch 2 (named as register) and Ch 3 (developed in operation as practice deepens with lunar / oracular work).
6. **The historical witch (§ X)** distributed across Ch 1 (orienting frame), Ch 3 (substantive reading and own lineage map building), Ch 4 (practitioner's actual location at depth).
