The Crossroads
and the Slow Door

A working guide to serious witchcraft and devotional practice across decades, structured as seven chapters that move through the lived shape of an evolving practice from before-the-beginning through the long view. Wide-survey across deities, traditions, and currents — Hellenic-chthonic, Welsh-Celtic, African Diasporic, Norse-Germanic, goddess-spirituality, Druidic, ceremonial-Hermetic, and reconstructionist polytheisms all treated as substantive territories rather than subordinate to a presumed center. The practitioner explores; specific commitment emerges from lived recognition or does not. The operative core (spellwork, divination, herbalism, trance, spirit work, the witch's calendar, grimoires) integrates across the spiral; druidry threads through as a parallel-tradition track keyed to the matrilineal Welsh-Celtic line.

— how to use this document —

This guide directs. It does not contain.

Each chapter opens as a catalog card and unfolds across multiple movements. The chapter prose is the spine; the apparatus around it does specific work. Read what calls. Practice what scales. Build what serves the long arc. Return when you have left.

Read
Reading lists distributed at the moments each text becomes relevant. Tiered Entry / Core / Depth; read in any order. The lists across all seven chapters target roughly 450+ texts at full build-out — beginner reads chapters 1-3 for the first six months; depth fills in across decades.
Practice
Specific practices scaled by time. Begin with the smallest tier you will actually do. Some practices ground at one chapter and develop across the rest of the document.
Build
Apparatus to construct yourself — the grimoire, the altar, the wheel calendar, the lunar log, the personal book of cards, the practitioner shortlist, the recognition record, the death-practice apparatus. The library is in the reading lists; the practice is what you build.
Research thread
Open scholarly questions and further-reading directions for territory the document does not develop. Threads to pull when ready.
Terminal interludes
System-state passages in monospace marking the practitioner's location at each chapter's threshold. The document acknowledging itself as a digital archive maintained over time.

The seven chapters

CH 01 entry · months 0–3 · return: year 1, year 5, always

Before the Beginning

position▪history▪lineage▪cautions▪academic foundations▪research practice▪the reading log

Orientation before any practice begins. Wide-entry frame, breadth-first discipline, lineage-by-recognition. The historical witch as orienting frame; the keyed reader's three-stream ancestral configuration named honestly; druidry as parallel-tradition track introduced; cautions held strictly. To be added: § I.IV academic foundations (Yates, Walker, Copenhaver, Hanegraaff, Faivre, Asprem); § I.V research practice (database literacy, ILL, UPG vs. attested, the reading journal); expanded witch-trial historiography. Reading log built before anything else. The container before what it holds.

First Workings

CH 04 entry · months 6–18 · return: year 3, year 7

The First Year

Apparatus running. The first complete turning of the wheel. The lunar architecture at depth. Body material surfacing as the regulation work earns the body's trust. CH 06 entry · year 3 → year 10 · return: year 14, always

The Deeper Years

recognition▪belief at depth▪commitment▪trance & journey▪grimoires at depth▪spirit work▪poison path▪excavation▪the work of repair▪ovate druidry

The spiral. Same territories at greater depths. Lineage commitment by recognition. The substantive philosophical engagement on belief with full apparatus. Body excavation with practitioner support. Teachers and community across the three streams. The work of repair as sustained practice. To be added: § VI.IX trance and the journey (automatic writing, deeper scrying, hedge-crossing — Ginzburg, Pócs, Wilby, Chumbley, Morgan, Artisson; Yeats, Jung's Red Book, af Klint, Tyson on automatic writing); § VI.X grimoire tradition at depth (Picatrix, Agrippa, Solomonic, Munich Manual, Greek Magical Papyri); § VI.XI spirit work distinct from ancestor work (genius loci, fae, named dead, saints); § VI.XII operative herbalism at depth (poison path with cautions); § VI.XIII witch-trial historiography as scholarly practice; ovate-grade druidry track.
CH 07 entry · year 10 onward · return: continuously

The Long View

What sustained practice over decades produces. Death practice as synthesis of decades of death-adjacent practice rather than introduction of new material. The contemporary contemplative apparatus alongside Welsh gwylnos, Gullah Geechee sit-up, alchemical death-and-rebirth. Teaching as kept-aside elder. Repair continued. To be added: § VII.VII the witch's calendar at depth (planetary hours, astrological timing as operative); druid-grade and elder-druid practice; § VII.VIII teaching operative practice (passing on the operative core); the synthesis of operative + devotional + ancestral across decades. The closing return to chapter 1's orientation from the other end.
